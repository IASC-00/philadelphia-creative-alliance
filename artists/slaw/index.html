Shaun “Slaw” Durbin

Philadelphia, PA

Philadelphia muralist. Designer of the Belfield Recreation Center interior murals.

Shaun Durbin paints as Slaw. He is a muralist in Philadelphia and is listed as an artist with Mural Arts Philadelphia.

He designed the interior murals at the Belfield Recreation Center in East Germantown (2022), a Mural Arts project. The murals show cartoon-like characters doing the things people do in and around the rec center.
