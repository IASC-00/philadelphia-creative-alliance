Jeilah

Chicago, IL

Taking work now

Kenya-born, Chicago-based DJ mixing disco, soul, reggae, rap, pop and afro house.

Jeilah is a DJ. She was born in Kenya and is based in Chicago.

She grew up around African music and dance and names Fela Kuti as an influence. Her sets mix disco and soul with reggae, rap, pop and afro house.

She has played Soho House, the Four Seasons and Celeste.

She also works as a model.

Three of her mixes are on SoundCloud: a disco demo mix, a cocktail mix and an afro mix.
