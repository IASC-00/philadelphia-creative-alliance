        Why an alliance

        The talent isn't missing. The connections are.

        Greater Philadelphia has no shortage of creative organizations. What it lacks is anything joining them up. Each one carries its own website, its own mailing list, its own promotion — usually on one overworked person — and a funder or client trying to understand the scene has no single place to look. Meanwhile the organization three neighborhoods over is solving the exact same problem from scratch.

        There is already a body that represents the sector, convenes it, and moves grant money through it. That isn't this. We don't lobby. We build. The Alliance is the working layer: a shared front door, referrals between members, and infrastructure that member organizations can actually pick up and use. No cut of anyone's work, no gatekeeping, and free for artists permanently.

        Who runs it

        Kept up by working studios.

        The Alliance runs through Gemini Strategic Consultants, a Philadelphia firm, which sets up members' pages and keeps them current. Our studios do the building. Both are operators, not members. They keep the wall up so member organizations don't have to.

        Keep it free

        Back the wall.

        Artist membership is free and stays free. Keeping it that way costs something — hosting, printing badges and stickers, and the time that goes into the wall.

        Money is the obvious way to help. It isn't the only one, and right now it isn't even the most useful one.
